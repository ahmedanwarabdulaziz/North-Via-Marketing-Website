SERVICE: Basic Business Process Review
URL: /services/basic-business-process-review

Hero:
  Headline: "Find the Bottlenecks. Fix the Flow."
  Description: "We analyze how your marketing, sales, and operations connect—
               then identify small process improvements that create big 
               business gains."
  Sub-description: "Simple, actionable insights that make your day-to-day run 
                   smoother and more profitably."

Problem Statement:
  "Why Many Businesses Plateau"
  
  "Growth often slows not because of poor marketing, but because internal 
   processes aren't built to scale. When workflows, tools, or communication 
   break down, results do too."

Common Challenges:
  • Leads fall through the cracks between marketing and sales
  • Repetitive manual work slows down response time
  • Inconsistent follow-up processes reduce conversions
  • No unified system for tracking performance
  • Owners spend more time fixing problems than driving growth

Our Solution:
  "We step into your business operations—observe, map, and simplify.
   We find the gaps that waste time, lose leads, and limit growth—then give 
   you a simple roadmap to fix them."

Review Process:
  1. Discovery Call - We discuss your goals, tools, and team structure.
  2. Process Mapping - We outline your existing customer journey and identify 
     weak spots.
  3. Gap Analysis - We locate missed opportunities, inefficiencies, or 
     duplicated work.
  4. Quick-Win Recommendations - You receive prioritized, low-effort 
     improvements that bring fast results.
  5. Action Plan Delivery - We summarize everything in a short, visual report 
     ready for immediate use.

What You Get:
  • Custom Workflow Diagram - Clear overview of your current process.
  • List of Bottlenecks & Inefficiencies - What's slowing you down.
  • Quick-Fix Recommendations - Top actions you can take immediately.
  • Integration Suggestions - Tools or automations to simplify operations.
  • Follow-Up Call - Review changes, next steps, and deeper consulting 
    options.

Results You Can Expect:
  • Faster Response Time - Smoother customer flow and quicker turnaround.
  • Less Wasted Time - Reduced manual or repetitive work.
  • Better Collaboration - Improved coordination between marketing and sales.
  • Clear Visibility - Better insight into your business operations.
  • Immediate Productivity Boost - Measurable improvements without major 
    system changes.

How We Measure Success:
  • Time Saved per Process - Measurable reduction in effort per task.
  • Lead Conversion Flow - Fewer dropped leads between marketing and sales.
  • Automation Readiness Score - How well your systems support scaling.
  • Team Feedback - Smoother internal communication and task ownership.

Included In:
  • Performance Partner: Single business process review + recommendations
  • Strategic Partner: In-depth multi-department review + implementation 
    guidance

Final CTA:
  "Simplify Your Systems. Multiply Your Results."
  "A one-hour review could save you dozens every month."

BUSINESS MARKETING ASSESSMENT (SURVEY)
================================================================================

Survey Name: Business Marketing Assessment
URL: /survey

Description:
  "Discover your digital marketing maturity level"
  
  A comprehensive assessment tool with:
  • 8 sections
  • 100 questions total
  • Takes about 10–15 minutes
  • Instant summary preview
  • Detailed report by email

Sections:
  1. Business Basics (Required)
     - Understanding your business structure, size, and decision-making process
     - 10 questions

  2. Website & Online Presence (Required)
     - Reviewing your website's performance, mobile-friendliness, and tracking 
       setup
     - 10 questions

  3. Social Media (Required)
     - Evaluating your social media presence, posting frequency, and 
       community engagement
     - 10 questions

  4. Advertising & Testing (Required)
     - Assessing your paid ads, testing programs, and analytics dashboards
     - 10 questions

  5. Customer Management (Optional)
     - Reviewing how you track customers, manage leads, and handle support
     - 10 questions

  6. Email & Automation (Optional)
     - Evaluating your email campaigns, automation workflows, and 
       personalization
     - 10 questions

  7. Strategy & Planning (Required)
     - Reviewing your marketing strategy, leadership support, and planning 
       processes
     - 10 questions

  8. Growth Mindset (Required)
     - Understanding your beliefs about marketing, willingness to adapt, and 
       growth goals
     - 16 questions

Scoring System:
  • Answers scored on 1–5 scale
  • Converted to 0–100 scale for display (e.g., 67/100)
  • Overall score calculated with weighted sections
  • Section-by-section breakdown provided

Maturity Levels:
  • Foundational (0–41/100)
  • Emerging (42–62/100)
  • Established (63–82/100)
  • Advanced (83–92/100)
  • Optimized (93–100/100)

Email Reports:
  • Customer receives: Personalized results summary with recommendations
  • Admin receives: Complete assessment with all details for follow-up
