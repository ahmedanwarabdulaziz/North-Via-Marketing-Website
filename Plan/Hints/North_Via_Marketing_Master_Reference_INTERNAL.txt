5) OPERATING MODEL
============================================================
Core Operating Idea:
North Via Marketing wins by giving small and medium businesses partner-level marketing support at a price point that is more affordable than what many local Canadian providers charge.

Why the model works:
- Lower operating cost base from Egypt
- Strong founder involvement
- Flexible support across multiple marketing needs
- Clients receive continuity, consultation, and execution in one relationship
- Especially attractive for owner-led businesses that cannot hire a full in-house team

Service Delivery Style:
- Social media posting and management
- Website updates and ongoing support
- Sponsored ads on social media platforms
- Google Ads support
- Marketing consultation and day-to-day guidance
- General business growth input where relevant

Relationship Model:
- Retainer-based
- High trust
- Founder-led
- Built through referrals and direct relationships
